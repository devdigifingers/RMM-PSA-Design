# Digital Fingers Invoicing: design notes for the mockups

By James (graphic design), 9 Nov 2026. These notes cover the mockups in this folder for spec §4 ("Templates that stand out") and §5 (email and hosted page).
**The spec itself was not edited.** Every change below says what I changed, why, and which spec line it touches, so Fiona can update the spec to match.
Line numbers refer to `/workspace/rmm/Digital-Fingers-Invoicing-Spec.md` as it was on 9 Nov 2026.

## Changes from the spec (what, why, spec line)

| # | Spec line(s) | Spec says | Mockup does | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | L297 (Bold), with L255 and §6 L452–456 | Bold title "INVOICE" | **"TAX INVOICE"** (44px Space Grotesk bold, white) | §4.1 L255 and §6 require "Tax Invoice" for a VAT vendor. The Bold rule conflicts with that. The title should follow the document kind on **every** template: TAX INVOICE / INVOICE / QUOTE / CREDIT NOTE. |
| 2 | L307 (Minimal) | Title "Invoice" | **"Tax invoice"** (20px regular, sentence case to keep the quiet look) | Same SARS reason as #1. |
| 3 | L297 (Bold) | Number at 70% white | **90% white** | 70% white on the primary colour is only 4.2:1 on DF violet and 3.5:1 on Umoya teal, which fails AA for 14px text. 90% white gives 6.0:1 and 4.75:1. The other secondary text in the footer band went from 80–85% to 90% for the same reason. |
| 4 | L295–297 (Bold) | Logo and title positions not stated | **Title and number on the left, white logo on the right** | The 135° gradient runs from primary (top left) to secondary (bottom right). Tenants' secondary colours are often light (DF cyan #0891B2 is 3.7:1 with white, Umoya lime is 3.1:1). So the text sits over the primary end, and only the logo sits over the secondary end. This keeps the text AA for any tenant pair. |
| 5 | L300 (Bold) | 14px row padding | **14px on screen, 12px in print** (`@media print`) | At 14px, six two-line rows plus the 140px header and the footer band don't fit on one A4 page. 12px still looks generous, and a typical monthly invoice stays on one page. |
| 6 | L277–278, with L242 and L253 | Total in a primary-colour band | Band is **primary → a "safe" gradient end** (`--grad-text-end`) | The client likes the ombre, so the total band carries it too. The gradient stops at the furthest primary→secondary mix that still gives white text ≥4.5:1 (computed in `common.py: tokens()`): #4975C1 for DF, #30845F for Umoya. The full primary→secondary run is kept for the bars that have no text: the 6px Modern top bar, the Bold header and the email header bar. |
| 7 | L264 | Gradient primary → secondary | Interpolated **in OKLab** (`linear-gradient(90deg in oklab, …)`) | sRGB mixing of violet → cyan goes muddy and greyish in the middle. OKLab keeps it clean and saturated. Chrome prints it as vector shading (no raster images in the PDFs). |
| 8 | L242 (brand colours) | Primary and secondary colour | DF tenant uses **primary #6D28D9, secondary #0891B2** | These are the "deeper gradient" pair from the Logo C README. The bright brand pair (#22D3EE / #7C3AED) can't carry text: cyan with white is 1.8:1. The ombre direction (violet → cyan) is the reverse of the mark's cyan → violet, which is fine because the spec defines primary → secondary. |
| 9 | L268–271 (Modern) | Labels 11px slate-500 | slate-500 #64748B kept on white (4.76:1). **slate-600 #475569 inside tinted cards** (Amount due card, MSP contract strip) | slate-500 on the 4% primary tint is 4.45:1, just under AA. |
| 10 | L270–272 | Amount due card | 268px wide, 12px radius, 4% tint **plus a 1px 14% tint border** | Without the border, the 4% tint almost disappears on paper and on low-contrast screens. |
| 11 | L279 (Modern footer) | How to pay / Terms / QR | Bank block condensed to **4 rows** ("Branch · type" on one row) | It fits A4 with six lines while keeping the bank, account name, number, branch and type, plus the reference chip. |
| 12 | L232 | Footer "Page X of Y" | Mockups print **"Page 1 of 1"** or "Page 1 of 2". The **hosted web view hides it.** | The mockups use fixed A4 page boxes. In production, the flowing template should use Chromium `page.pdf` `footerTemplate` (`.pageNumber` / `.totalPages`) or CSS `@page` margin boxes with `counter(page)` / `counter(pages)`. |
| 13 | L312 (Minimal) | Footer is "one quiet line" | **Two quiet lines** (EFT details + reference, then account name and online link) next to a 44px QR | Bank, account number, branch, reference, account name and link don't fit on one line at a readable size (10.5px). |
| 14 | L286 (Classic) | Address + VAT in one 11px line | Kept as one line for DF. **Allow a 2nd line** for long addresses. | Address, phone, VAT and reg. no. is about 110 characters, close to the 682px content width. |
| 15 | L288 (Classic) | Table headings | Last column is **"Amount (excl.)"** | It makes the VAT treatment explicit for bookkeepers (§6 item 7). |
| 16 | L291 (Classic) | "Authorised signature" line | Signature line **plus a "Date" line** | This is the usual layout on SA accounting documents. Both lines are optional. |
| 17 | L317, L324 (MSP) | Contract strip shows the period (1–30 Nov 2026); page 2 is "this month" | The strip adds **"Usage billed: 1–31 Oct 2026"**. Page 2 says **"October 2026 (1–31 Oct)"**. | Recurring fees are billed in advance (November), while support time and activity are billed in arrears (October). Without this, "this month" is ambiguous. **Spec should say: page 2 reports the usage period, not the contract period.** |
| 18 | L320 (MSP support time) | Included hours used, overage × rate | An **"Included support hours: 10 of 10 h, Included, R 0,00"** row above the overage row | The customer sees the allowance was used before overage was charged. |
| 19 | L326 (usage bars) | Green / amber / red | Over 100%: **red bar at full width, with a dark tick at the included limit**, plus a chip "115% · 1,5 h over" | A bar can't exceed 100% of its track, so the tick shows where the allowance ended. The devices bar uses the same colour rules (green when covered = billed). |
| 20 | L330 (AI summary) | Marked "Reviewed by {technician}" | Byline: **"Summary drafted with AI · Reviewed by Sipho Ndlovu, 1 Nov 2026"** | It's open about the AI. Placed first on page 2, it gives the context for the numbers below it. |
| 21 | L325, L327–329 (page 2) | KPI tiles, top tickets, remote sessions, devices | Each section shows a **small suite app icon as its data source** (Service Desk, Remote Desk, Monitoring) | This reinforces the suite value. Per the UI spec §10, app hues are not used for statuses. The footer states "Internal notes are never included" (L332). |
| 22 | §4.1 L231 | Screen view: 820px card | **Mobile (<600px) rules added** (none in spec): the info row stacks; line rows become description + amount, with a "qty × unit · VAT 15%" sub-line; the doc's own Amount due card is hidden because the page hero shows it; the footer columns stack | The hosted page has to work on phones. It's the same markup with CSS only (`modern.css` media query), so "one template, two outputs" still holds. |
| 23 | §4.1 L233 | Fonts self-hosted, embedded | Use **static font instances** (e.g. `Inter-400.ttf` … `Inter-800.ttf`) for PDF rendering | Chrome embeds *variable* fonts as Type 3 in `page.pdf`. They print but aren't ideal for text extraction or accessibility. The static instances embed as TrueType (CID). They're generated with fontTools in `src/assets/fonts/`. |
| 24 | §5.1 L374 | Subject "…– R12 650,00 due 30 Nov" | **"Invoice INV-000123 from Digital Fingers – R 12 650,00 due 30 Nov"** | It matches the R 12 650,00 format used everywhere else: a non-breaking space after R and as the thousands separator. The spec example also names Compu-Extravaganza; it should use `{tenant.name}`. |
| 25 | §5.1 L369 | Branded HTML email | Header bar uses `background:<primary>` then `background-image:linear-gradient(…)` | Outlook ignores CSS gradients, so it falls back to the solid primary colour. The logo must be an **absolute PNG URL** (mockup uses the local 480w PNG). |
| 26 | §5.1 L372 | Big View invoice + Pay now | **View invoice is filled (primary). Pay now is an outlined secondary button.** Both stack full-width on mobile. | It gives one clear primary action. A short EFT line with the reference is added for customers who pay by EFT. |
| 27 | §5.3 L405–414 | Hosted page contents | **Desktop:** 820px document card plus a 1fr sidebar (Pay now card, Pay by EFT with copy buttons and a reference box, proof-of-payment upload, Query this invoice, Payments). Top actions: Download PDF / Print / Query. **Mobile:** amount + Pay now first, then Download PDF / Query, then the document, EFT, proof upload, the **open query form** (name, message) and payments. | This follows §5.3 and §8.3 (copy buttons, "Use reference", "I've paid – upload proof of payment"). The query panel says "Reminders pause while your query is open" (§5.5 L433). |

## Round 2: non-VAT issuer, statuses, statement, credit note, continuation (9 Nov 2026)
| # | Spec ref | Spec says | Mockup does | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 28 | §4 (tenant data) | Mockups assumed a VAT vendor | **Add `tenant.vat_registered: boolean`** (plus nullable `vat_number`). Every VAT difference below keys off this one flag, and templates never test `vat_number` for truthiness. `totals()` returns `{subtotal, vat: 0, total}` for non-VAT tenants, so the stored data model is identical for both. | Lets VAT and non-VAT tenants share all five templates. A VAT number without the flag, or the flag without a number, should be a settings validation error. |
| 29 | §4.1 / SARS | Title "TAX INVOICE" | **Title rule:** `vat_registered` gives "TAX INVOICE". Otherwise it's "INVOICE" (Classic letter-spaced "INVOICE", Minimal "Invoice"). Credit notes are "CREDIT NOTE" for both (a VAT vendor's credit note is a tax credit note; the title can stay CREDIT NOTE). Statements are "STATEMENT". | A non-VAT vendor must not issue a "tax invoice" (VAT Act s20). |
| 30 | §4.1 line table | Description · Qty · Unit · VAT · Amount | **Columns:** for non-VAT tenants the VAT column is **removed** (not blanked or zero). Description widens to fill. On mobile the sub-line is "14 × R 320,00" without "· VAT 15%". Line amounts are the final amounts. | Showing a "0%" or "—" column implies VAT applies. |
| 31 | §4.1 totals | Subtotal (excl. VAT) / VAT 15% / Total (incl. VAT) | **Totals:** a non-VAT tenant shows a single grand row **"Total R 11 000,00"** (no "excl."/"incl." words), then a small 10px slate note **"Not registered for VAT"**, then "Balance due". The Amount due card, email table and hosted hero show the same total. VAT tenants are unchanged (06). | "excl. VAT" would suggest VAT is still to be added. The note answers the question an accountant will ask. |
| 32 | §4.1 supplier block, §5.1 email | Supplier VAT no. | **Supplier block:** for non-VAT tenants the VAT no. line is omitted and the Reg. no. stays. The customer's VAT no. is still shown when the customer has one. **Email subject:** "Invoice INV-000123 from {tenant.trading_name} – R 11 000,00 due 30 Nov". The sender display name is the legal name, and reply-to is the tenant billing email. The footer stays "Sent with Digital Fingers Invoicing". | The customer's VAT number is their data, not the issuer's status. |
| 33 | §4.1 L232 (overflow) | Table header repeats | **Continuation (11):** page 1 keeps the full header and ends the table with a tinted **"Carried forward to page 2 R 133 804,00"** row. It has a **slim footer** (1px rule, "Continued on page 2 →" in primary-text 11.5px/600 left, "{trading} · INV-… · Page 1 of 2" right) and **no payment block**. Pages 2+ start with a **52px compact header** (logo 28px; "**INVOICE** INV-000124 (continued)" 13px; customer + issued date; "Page 2 of 2" right; 1px bottom rule), the repeated table head, a "Brought forward" row, then the remaining lines, totals and the full payment footer **on the last page only**. A group split across pages repeats its label on the next page (without "(continued)"). Long invoices use dense rows (5px vertical padding). | It saves ~90px per continuation page compared with repeating the full header. Payment details on every page invite paying a partial amount. In production, compute carried-forward per page in the renderer. |
| 34 | §4.1 L259 (stamps) | Diagonal status stamp | **Stamp spec (07):** Inter 800, uppercase, +0.08em tracking. **120px** for one word, **76px** for two (PARTIALLY PAID). Frame is a 0.06em border with 0.14em radius, in the same colour. Rotated **−24°**, centred at 50% / 46% of the page. **10% opacity** (text + frame), above content, `pointer-events:none`, `aria-hidden`. Colours: PAID #15803D, PARTIALLY PAID #B45309, OVERDUE #B91C1C, DRAFT #475569, VOID #111827. | It's readable as a watermark without hurting the legibility of the figures under it. |
| 35 | §4.1 / §5.3 statuses | — | **Pills:** Inter 700 11px, +0.08em, 999px radius, 5px × 10px padding. Pairs: PAID #DCFCE7/#166534, PARTIALLY PAID #FEF3C7/#8A4508, OVERDUE #FEE2E2/#991B1B, DRAFT #F1F5F9/#334155, VOID #E5E7EB/#111827 (all ≥ 6.4:1). The pill sits **inline before the document number** under the title. **Where stamps show:** the originally issued PDF is never altered. PAID / PARTIALLY PAID / VOID stamps appear only on a regenerated **"Copy"** PDF (footer starts "COPY ·", per §5.3 L408) and on the hosted page. **OVERDUE is hosted page + reminder only, never printed.** **DRAFT always shows** (PDF preview, PDF, hosted). | An issued tax document must stay byte-identical (audit trail). A printed "OVERDUE" goes stale the moment it is paid. |
| 36 | §4.1 (paid copy, 08) | — | **Paid copy:** PAID stamp + pill "Paid · 14 Nov 2026". Totals add a payment row "**Paid 14 Nov 2026 · EFT −R 11 000,00**" and then "Balance due R 0,00".
